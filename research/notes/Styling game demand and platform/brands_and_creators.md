# Brands and Creators for a Mid-Market Gen Z Styling Game (as of Nov 2026)

Research note: about 18 tool calls. Many brand revenue figures come from third-party estimators (ECDB, Growjo), not company disclosures, and are flagged. Anything I could not source is under Gaps. Researched 2026-11-21.

## 1. Small/mid-size mid-market Gen Z brands: size, programs, gaming history

### Takeaway
The clearest candidate tier is DTC "TikTok-native" labels with roughly $100M–$250M in estimated GMV: Princess Polly (~$250M est.), Oh Polly (~$240M est.), Edikted (~$120–131M est.). They are big enough to matter and small enough to want exposure. Most run standard affiliate programs (e.g., Princess Polly on Rakuten, ~5%). I found no evidence that any of the named small/mid brands has done a Covet, Drest, or Zepeto-style game collaboration, so a game partnership would be a first for them.

### Cited Findings
**Princess Polly (AU/US, a.k.a. Brands)**
- Largest brand in a.k.a. Brands, about half of company revenue. Double-digit net sales growth in 2025, driven by DTC and omnichannel — [Motley Fool Q4 2025 transcript, Mar 5 2026](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/03/05/aka-brands-aka-q4-2025-earnings-transcript); [Insider Monkey Q4 2025 transcript](https://www.insidermonkey.com/blog/a-k-a-brands-holding-corp-nyseaka-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript-1711379/)
- Opened 7 US stores in 2025 (13 US total at year end) plus a first AU store at Bondi. Plans 8–10 more in 2026. Launched in all Nordstrom stores in March 2025 — [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/03/05/aka-brands-aka-q4-2025-earnings-transcript)
- TikTok Shop revenue up 60% YoY (per an a.k.a. earnings call, 2025) — [Yahoo Finance Q4 call summary](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/k-brands-q4-earnings-call-060800191.html)
- ECDB estimates 2025 GMV at US$250M, with 0–5% growth forecast for 2026. This is a third-party estimate that conflicts with management's "double-digit" growth — [ECDB](https://ecdb.com/resources/sample-data/retailer/princesspolly)
- Affiliate program runs on Rakuten Advertising, ~5% commission, with separate US/UK/AU programs (directory data, may be stale) — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/princess-polly-affiliate-program/); [UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-program-directory/princess-polly). The brand's own "partner with us" page — [Princess Polly](https://us.princesspolly.com/pages/partner-with-us)

**Edikted (US, LA-based)**
- ECDB estimates edikted.com revenue at US$131M in 2025, with 15–20% growth forecast. Growjo estimates ~$119.2M. Both are unverified estimates — [ECDB](https://ecdb.com/resources/sample-data/retailer/edikted); [Growjo](https://growjo.com/company/Edikted)
- Has a SoHo NYC store next to Princess Polly's flagship (Apr 2025) — [Retail TouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/topics/inventory-merchandising/with-store-expansion-on-the-agenda-princess-polly-doubles-down-on-its-trendsetter-reputation)
- Edikted content is tagged in ShopMy creator directories. That shows creators promote it there, but it does not confirm an official program — [influencers.club ShopMy fashion](https://influencers.club/top-affiliates/shopmy/fashion/)

**Oh Polly (UK)**
- ECDB estimates 2025 GMV at US$240M (+10–15% YoY), with 5–10% growth forecast for 2026. Unverified estimate — [ECDB](https://ecdb.com/resources/sample-data/retailer/oh-polly)

**Motel Rocks (UK)**
- Growjo describes it as a "leading women's fashion label for the youth market". No reliable revenue figure found — [Growjo](https://growjo.com/company/Motel_Rocks)

**Lulus (US)**
- Has an affiliate program for bloggers and influencers. Network and commission not confirmed — [ReferralCandy roundup](https://www.referralcandy.com/blog/fashion-affiliate-programs)

**Cider**
- Search results for "Cider" valuation pointed to a Hong Kong consumer company "valued at $1 billion in Sep 2021". It is likely the same Cider fast-fashion brand, but I could not confirm it from this source — [Premier Alts](https://www.premieralts.com/companies/cider/funding-history); [Caplight](https://www.caplight.com/company/cider)

**Inclusive-size / petite**
- Good American: $200M annual sales in 2022 (company-confirmed). 2026 sales up 30% YoY (per Glossy). Declined to share 2025 revenue. ECDB's estimate of $42M (2025) conflicts with this and looks like a domain-only figure — [Glossy, 10 years in](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/10-years-in-good-american-brings-its-full-vision-to-nyc-with-landmark-pop-up/); [ECDB](https://ecdb.com/resources/sample-data/retailer/goodamerican)
- Universal Standard: strong response to a collection with influencer Abisola Omole shows demand for extended sizing. eMarketer groups it with Quince as retailers serving that demand — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/universal-standard-proves-demand-extended-sizing-remains-strong)

**Creator programs at larger mid-market brands (benchmarks)**
- Aerie's "Realmakers" creator community got 10,000+ applications in its first two weeks, and creators commit to no retouching or AI — [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/aerie-ups-commitment-to-authenticity-with-enhanced-creator-program/818404/)
- Gap launched a multi-brand affiliate and advocacy platform for creators — [Retail TouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/?p=154796)
- Creator programs are going mainstream among retailers chasing social commerce — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/creator-programs-go-mainstream-retailers-chase-social-fueled-commerce)
- LTK sued ShopMy in May 2024 (false advertising and trademark claims). Both remain the main fashion creator-affiliate platforms — [ReferralCandy](https://www.referralcandy.com/blog/fashion-affiliate-programs); context on affiliate platforms and micro-influencers — [Fashionista, Dec 2025](https://fashionista.com/2025/12/affiliate-platforms-evolution-substack-micro-influencers-2)

### Inferences
- Princess Polly, Edikted, and Oh Polly already lean on TikTok Shop and stores, so a styling game is an extra discovery channel for them. A pitch based on affiliate revenue share (e.g., 5% via Rakuten or ShopMy links in-game) could fit their existing systems, so they would not need to buy media.
- Since none of these brands appears to have done a gaming deal, being first is a selling point ("first playable catalog").
- Good American's 30% growth in 2026 and the extended-sizing demand signals support including at least one inclusive-size brand. That would also help with the body-type styling creators (Section 3).

### Gaps

These are not searched and unverified from memory: Lulus is publicly listed (NASDAQ: LVLU), Abercrombie and Aritzia are public with multi-billion revenue, and Vuori and Quince have reported large private valuations. Verify before citing.
- Affiliate networks for most brands (Edikted, Motel, Hello Molly, Dissh, etc.) were not confirmed. Check each brand's footer "affiliates" page or the ShopMy/LTK brand directories.
- Price ranges per brand were not collected from primary sources. Check the brands' own sites.

## 2. Brands already working with games / virtual fashion

### Takeaway
Real-catalog styling games historically used big or contemporary brands (Covet had 150+), but Covet has since dropped real brands for its own labels. Drest is luxury-only. Roblox is where mid-market and athleisure brands find scale. Alo is the standout success (118M visits). Forever 21's store-sim is the cautionary tale (250K visits in year one).

### Cited Findings
- **Covet Fashion** (Crowdstar, launched Nov 12, 2013 → Glu Mobile acquired Crowdstar for $45M in 2016 → EA acquired Glu in 2021). It featured 150+ real brands but now uses only Covet-branded clothing. Wikipedia gives no date or reason for the change. Claimed ~2M monthly players in 2020. Players vote between looks for star ratings — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covet_Fashion)
- Covet's past partners were contemporary rather than Gen Z mid-market: Badgley Mischka, Rachel Zoe (2019), J Brand, Cynthia Rowley, French Connection, Nicole Miller, BCBGMAXAZRIA, Rebecca Minkoff — [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/fashion-brands-are-linking-with-gaming-apps-to-acquire-customers); [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/move-kardashian-mobile-game-striking-chord-women-retailers); [Fortune, Sep 2015](https://fortune.com/2015/09/09/covet-fashion-mobile-game/)
- In May 2025 Covet (EA) added the digital-native brand DRESSX. That suggests digital-only brand partners are still accepted — [EA Covet news](https://www.ea.com/games/covet-fashion/covet-fashion/news/step-into-the-future-with-new-brand-dressx)
- **Drest** partners are luxury: Breitling, Cartier (Mar 2026 Clash de Cartier), Christian Louboutin, Fendi, Gucci, Prada, Valentino, Versace, Pandora, Kate Moss, and Copenhagen Fashion Week. Its closest non-luxury partner is BAKEUP (digital-first beauty). Revenue comes from IAP, brand partnerships, ads, and branded "quests" — [FashionUnited, Mar 2026](https://fashionunited.com/news/fashion/drest-continues-expansion-into-luxury-jewellery-with-cartier/2026031171088); [PocketGamer.biz BAKEUP](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/drest-to-partner-with-bakeup-for-in-game-collaboration); [Glossy, mobile fashion gaming](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/mobile-fashion-gaming-is-gaining-traction/)
- **Alo Yoga on Roblox:** "Alo Sanctuary" (2022), with a major update on Jan 29, 2025. 118M visits since 2022, users roughly doubled YoY, and called the "fastest-growing brand" on Roblox. NFC stickers in its 150 stores unlock a free digital jacket, linking stores to the game. At one point it sold more digital leggings than physical ones. Digital items cost 70–148 Robux. Promoted via TikTok, IG, and Roblox ads. Spend undisclosed — [Glossy, Jan 2025](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/fashion-briefing-how-alo-yoga-is-using-roblox-to-get-gen-z-to-its-stores)
- **Forever 21 "Shop City" on Roblox** (Dec 2021, built by Virtual Brand Group + Wonder Works): a store-management sim. About 250K visits in year one, vs Nikeland at 28M and Tommy Play at 26.5M. Some teens found it underwhelming. It held "Forever 21 Day" monthly events. It was the first brand to sell physical versions of its Roblox items (2022) — [Inverse/Input](https://www.inverse.com/input/gaming/forever-21-shop-city-roblox-game-teens-dislike); [Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/forever-21s-metaverse-venture-lets-roblox-users-customize-virtual-fashion/611779/); [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/fashion-briefing-how-alo-yoga-is-using-roblox-to-get-gen-z-to-its-stores)
- Roblox added a feature for selling physical products in Sept 2024. e.l.f. was among the users — [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/fashion-briefing-how-alo-yoga-is-using-roblox-to-get-gen-z-to-its-stores)
- DKNY launched virtual products on Roblox in May 2024 — [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.com/news/retail/dkny-launches-exclusive-heart-of-new-york-virtual-products-on-roblox/2024050259691). Puma partnered with DRESSX's "DressGo" Roblox game instead of building its own world — [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/dressx-roblox-game-puma/753347/)
- Agency-built Roblox worlds cost about $10K–$100K. VLGE tooling lowers that, and 50 brands launched shoppable worlds for a fashion week event — [TechCrunch, Oct 17 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/17/vlge-is-making-it-easier-to-world-build-and-shop-on-roblox/)
- Forever 21, Burberry, and Tommy Hilfiger hired Roblox UGC creators (e.g., @builder_boy as Forever 21's "Metaverse Fashion Consultant"). No metrics given (Jul 2022) — [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/fashion-brands-are-competing-for-roblox-creators/)
- **Zepeto:** DRESSX was the first digital fashion retailer partner (Sept 2022). Coach launched virtual items on Zepeto and Roblox (2024). Other collaborations include Dior and Christian Louboutin (luxury), and NARS and e.l.f. (beauty) — [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.com/news/business/dressx-becomes-first-digital-fashion-retailer-to-partner-with-zepeto/2022090149461); [Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/roblox/coach); [Cosmetics Business](https://cosmeticsbusiness.com/elf-cosmetics-partners-with-zepeto-virtual-styling)

### Inferences
- Covet dropping real brands suggests that brand-permission catalogs are costly to maintain (licensing, item churn). The founder should plan for SKU turnover and image-rights workflows from day one.
- Alo's results came from a game loop (wellness activities) plus a physical-retail hook, not a store simulator. Forever 21's store-sim underperformed. A styling game with voting is closer to Covet/Alo-style engagement than to a branded store.
- No Gen Z mid-market DTC brand (Princess Polly, Edikted, etc.) showed up in these partnership lists. That gap is the opportunity.

### Gaps
- Found no mid-market brand deals in Fortnite or Animal Crossing with public results. Abercrombie/Hollister and American Eagle on Roblox were not confirmed by this search.
- No public ROI data (sales lift, customer acquisition cost) for Covet brand partners.

## 3. Creators reaching this audience + micro-influencer rates

### Takeaway
The strongest creator pools for this game are body-type niches (midsize, petite, plus) and Amazon/budget-finds creators, usually 50K–650K followers. Planning rates for 10K–100K-follower micro creators run roughly $500–$1,500 per IG post and $800–$2,500 per Reel/TikTok (Later, Jul 2026). Influencer Marketing Hub quotes lower figures ($150–$500 IG, $200–$800 TikTok). Vendor figures vary by 3–10x.

### Cited Findings
**Rate benchmarks (2026)**
- Later (Jul 7, 2026): Nano (1K–10K) $100–$300 IG feed, $200–$500 Reel/TikTok, $50–$150 Stories. Micro (10K–100K) $500–$1,500 IG feed, $800–$2,500 Reel/TikTok, $1,000–$3,000+ bundle with usage rights. Macro (100K–1M) $2,500–$10,000+. Reels add 20–30%, Stories run about 50% below baseline, whitelisting adds 30–50%. TikTok pricing follows average views. The rate data source is not disclosed — [Later](https://later.com/blog/influencer-pricing-benchmarks-the-complete-2026-guide/)
- Influencer Marketing Hub 2026: micro IG ~$150–$500 per feed post, micro TikTok ~$200–$800 per video — [IMH micro rates](https://influencermarketinghub.com/micro-influencer-rates/); [IMH influencer rates 2026](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-rates/)
- Other ranges: Shopify TikTok micro $200–$1,200 — [Shopify](https://shopify.com/blog/influencer-pricing). ContentGrip IG micro $500–$5,000 — [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/influencer-marketing-rate-card/). Meltwater IG $100–$5,000, TikTok $25–$1,250 — [Meltwater](https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/influencer-marketing-costs-rates-pricing)

**Creator types and example public handles (follower counts are from profile pages and may be stale)**
- Midsize: an IZEA roundup names a sizes 12–14 creator (~78.7K TikTok), a midsize stylist (~647K TikTok), and an Amazon-finds creator (~438K TikTok) — [IZEA midsize influencers](https://izea.com/resources/midsize-influencers/)
- Midsize/petite: Logan Rae Hill (sizes 6/8), Abi Nunn (petite size 12), Madison Beltran (~153K) — [Her Campus](https://www.hercampus.com/?p=225974). Abby Geter (5'0" petite + midsize, affordable fashion) — [The Social Cat](https://thesocialcat.com/influencers/midsize-fashion)
- Plus-size / budget Amazon finds: Nahiely Alexandra, @eclectickurves (Sierra), featuring items like a $48.99 velvet wrap dress — [Yahoo/StyleCaster](https://www.yahoo.com/lifestyle/tiktok-fashion-influencers-obsessed-plus-210040570.html). @whosthatgirldana (~66K) — [The Social Cat plus-size](https://thesocialcat.com/influencers/plus-size-fashion). "Curvy coaches" such as Phaith Montoya show fit realities, including items that didn't fit — [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/tiktok-made-them-style-stars-200000393.html)
- "Fit-fluencers" have pushed brands on size inclusivity (2021) — [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/calling-brands-out-how-tiktoks-fit-fluencers-are-forwarding-size-inclusivity-in-fashion)
- Universal Standard × Abisola Omole collab drew strong response — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/universal-standard-proves-demand-extended-sizing-remains-strong)

### Inferences
- Paying 10 micro creators at Later's midpoint (~$1,500 per TikTok) costs about $15K per wave, or about $5K at IMH rates. Budget for usage rights if clips are reused as ads (+30–50%).
- Body-type creators fit a recreate-the-look game well: "style this on a petite or midsize body" can be a challenge category, and those creators have a built-in reason to post.

### Gaps
- No CreatorIQ or Collabstr 2026 rate data retrieved.
- No verified list of 2026 "outfit formula" or capsule-wardrobe creators with current follower counts. Check TikTok Creative Center or ShopMy/LTK directories.

## 4. Hashtags and content formats with most reach

### Takeaway
Dupe content has huge hashtag scale (#dupe at roughly 4.3–6B TikTok views, 2023–25 snapshots). About half of Gen Z have knowingly bought a dupe. GRWM still breaks out at about the same rate as other fashion video, but its volume and engagement fell in 2025. Fashion TikTok engagement averages about 2.4–3.0% of views.

### Cited Findings
- #dupe TikTok views reported at 4.3B to ~6B, depending on article date (2023–2025). Morning Consult found ~49% of Gen Z and 44% of millennials have intentionally bought a dupe, vs 31% of all adults — [CNBC, Oct 2023](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/10/31/gen-z-millennials-are-shopping-for-dupes-the-most-report-finds.html); [Morning Consult](https://pro.morningconsult.com/analysis/dupe-buyer-demographics-brand-strategy); [Salon, Jan 2025](https://www.salon.com/2025/01/14/bye-bye-birkin-gen-z-leans-into-the-dupe-economy)
- Gen Z and millennials look for "best dupes" on TikTok (50%), Facebook (48%), Instagram (42%), and YouTube (42%), per Trustpilot. 30–49% were disappointed with dupes they bought — [Retail Brew, Jun 2023](https://retailbrew.com/stories/2023/06/20/gen-z-luxury-dupes)
- Fashion TikTok engagement is ~2.4% (down from 2.9%), with views up 13% and average retention 23%. Top brands exceed 8% (Dash Social 2026) — [Dash Social fashion benchmarks](https://www.dashsocial.com/social-media-benchmarks/fashion-industry). The H2 2025 report gave 3.0% with views up 33% — [Dash H2 2025 PDF](https://pages.dashsocial.com/hubfs/social-media-benchmark/2025/H2/fashion-industry.pdf)
- GRWM (Oct–Nov 2026, Virlo): 23.7% of 531 fashion GRWM videos beat their account's usual views by 2x+, vs 23.1% for all fashion videos. Breakout GRWMs ran a median of 73s, vs 51s for all GRWMs — [Virlo](https://virlo.ai/tiktok-ideas/fashion/grwm)
- GRWM fatigue: posts down 10% YoY, engagements down 19%, views down 17% (Traackr 2025, beauty+fashion) — [Traackr](https://www.traackr.com/blog/grwm-beauty-creator-marketing-fatigue)
- Narrative GRWM and "aesthetic silence" had the highest engagement in Draper's sample (vendor blog, unverified methods) — [Draper](https://draper.chat/blog/tiktok-fashion-hooks-2026)
- Monthly breakdown of top fashion TikTok content (Mar 2025) — [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/fashion/a-breakdown-of-fashions-top-performing-tiktok-content-in-march/2025040981003)

### Inferences
- "Recreate the look for less" maps directly onto dupe-vs-original content, which has the biggest hashtag scale. Vote-based "rate my outfit" results can be turned into shareable clips. Longer narrative GRWMs (~70s) seem to outperform short ones.
- GRWM is saturating, so in-game formats like "styling one piece 5 ways" or "outfit formula" challenges may stand out more. That is not verified by benchmark data.
- Dupe framing carries brand-relations risk. Partner brands may not want to be shown as the "dupe" or the "original". Framing it as "the look for under $X" is safer.

### Gaps
- No verified view counts found for #outfitformula, #stylingonepiece, #ratemyoutfit, #midsizefashion, #under200, or #capsulewardrobe. Check TikTok Creative Center directly.
- No format-level engagement benchmark found for "dupe vs original" or "X ways to style" videos. The Benly and dev.to tables cite no sources and were excluded.
