# Audience: Who Needs Affordable Styling Help, and Who Plays Styling Games (as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-21. About 19 tool calls. Many primary dashboards (Sensor Tower country splits, full McKinsey/BoF reports, YPulse) are paywalled, so the figures below come from public summaries. Every figure is dated, and estimates or secondary-source figures are flagged.

## 1. Demographics of people seeking styling help, consuming outfit/dupe content, and buying mid-market/fast fashion

### Takeaway
The core is young women: US teen girls and women aged 18–35. They are price-first (price is the #1 factor for about 66% of Gen Z) but over-index on design and "fashionable", and they get inspiration on Pinterest (42% Gen Z, mostly female) and TikTok. Teen apparel spend rebounded in fall 2026, led by girls and higher-income teens, which shows demand is not only budget-driven.

### Cited Findings
- **Piper Sandler 26th annual teen survey, fall 2026** (published about Oct 21 2026; nearly 9,000 US teens aged 13–19):
  - Spending: about $2,350 per teen per year; total teen spend about $65B; overall spend up 6% YoY, up 11% among higher-income teens.
  - Apparel and girls: apparel spend up 7% YoY and girls' apparel up 10%. Clothing is 21–22% of teen wallet. Teen girls' apparel and accessories were each up 13% YoY.
  - Brands and retailers: specialty retailers' share of fashion spend is 23% (up 8 pts); athletic brands are at 29% (from a peak of 44% three years earlier). Brandy Melville, Alo, Ugg and Birkenstock are popular with girls. Coach leads girls' handbags at 60%. Target is the apparel store for 50% of upper-income girls. Amazon is about 50% of upper-income teens' online share.
  - Secondhand: 44% of upper-income teens buy secondhand clothing.
  - Economy: 70% of teens say the economy is getting worse (62% in fall 2025).
  - The article does not mention Shein or Temu. — [Retail Dive, Oct 2026](https://retaildive.com/news/teen-spending-grows-trade-athletic-apparel-fashion-piper-sandler/833565)
- **Piper Sandler sample discrepancy:** The public Piper Sandler page still showed fall 2025 results (10,969 teens) while 2026 headlines cited about 9,000 teens. In fall 2025, Shein appeared in the top clothing rankings but lost ground with upper-income female and male teens. — [roic.ai, Oct 13 2026](https://www.roic.ai/news/piper-sandler-teen-survey-tiktok-roblox-and-beauty-lead-as-snapchat-and-netflix-lag-10-13-2026); [Piper Sandler Fall 2025 infographic](https://www.pipersandler.com/sites/default/files/document/TS_Fall25_Infographic.pdf)
- **YouGov, Gen Z vs all US adults** (article Mar 2 2026; data Feb 2025–Feb 2026):

  | Measure | Gen Z | All US adults |
  |---|---|---|
  | Price is a top factor | 66% | 69% |
  | Value for money | 56% | 62% |
  | Good fit | 56% | 57% |
  | Design (color and style) | 41% | 33% |
  | "Fashionable" | 26% | 19% |
  | Sustainability | 18% | 13% |
  | Shop in a clothing store at least monthly | 22% | 11% |
  | Browse in stores | 51% | 42% |
  | Saw online, then bought in-store | 15% | 10% |

  — [YouGov, Mar 2026](https://yougov.com/en-us/articles/54192-gen-z-apparel-shopping-what-sets-the-younger-generation-apart-from-the-overall-population)
- **McKinsey/BoF State of Fashion 2026** (Nov 2025):
  - Gen Z spending is growing about 2x as fast as earlier generations and is on track to pass boomers by 2029.
  - 59% of consumers globally plan to buy secondhand in 2026.
  - 46% of executives expect conditions to worsen (39% the prior year). — [McKinsey newsletter, Dec 2025](https://www.mckinsey.com/~/media/mckinsey/email/genz/2025/12/2025-12-02a.html); [McKinsey DE press release, Nov 18 2025](https://www.mckinsey.com/de/news/presse/2025-11-18-state-of-fashion); [McKinsey takeaways](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/retail/our-takeaways/state-of-fashion)
- **Dupes (secondary, not verified against the primary report):** State of Fashion 2025 reportedly found 54% of consumers buy a cheaper version or dupe and 46% shop at cheaper brands. No Gen Z-specific number was given. — [Aurelia Ammour Substack citing SoF 2025](https://aureliaammour.substack.com/p/fashion-value-driven-shopping-behaviors)
- **BCG (Oct 2025):** 40% of Gen Z use AI tools to shop. Gen Z prioritizes products over brands, with price and quality as the fundamentals. — [BCG PDF](https://web-assets.bcg.com/f2/87/c4b4ec234d9d9e8b43fc4119bd11/how-gen-z-and-gen-alpha-are-rewiring-the-fashion-industry.pdf)
- **Pinterest audience:**
  - Gen Z is 42% of global monthly users.
  - In 2024, Gen Z fashion searches grew YoY: men's fashion +35%, women's fashion +30%.
  - Finding shopping information is the #1 reason Gen Z uses Pinterest, and Gen Z clicks and saves on shoppable Pins rose 86% YoY (2024). — [Pinterest Business blog, 2024](https://business.pinterest.com/blog/gen-z-marketing-on-pinterest)
- **Pinterest scale and gender (sources conflict):** Hootsuite reports 631M MAU (Q1 2026), Gen Z as the fastest-growing audience, and an audience that is about 70% female. Pinterest itself says men are now more than a third of its users. — [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/pinterest-statistics/); [Mediaweek](https://mediaweek.com.au/pinterest-sees-male-audience-surge-launches-pinterest-man-hub)
- **BoF Insights:** In a survey of nearly 1,000 US Gen Z, 89% rate fashion as important for boosting confidence. — [BoF Insights, Gen Z Fashion in the Age of Realism](https://Businessoffashion.com/reports/retail/gen-z-fashion-in-the-age-of-realism-bof-insights-social-media-report/)

### Inferences
- **Primary US persona:** a woman aged 16–28 who is price-first but design-forward. She shops at specialty, mid-market and mass retailers (Target, Amazon, Brandy-tier, secondhand) and uses Pinterest and TikTok as her styling search engine. YouGov's "design 41% vs 33%" gap is the clearest public evidence that Gen Z wants looks, not just low prices.
- **Teen data is a leading indicator only.** Piper Sandler covers 13–19-year-olds; most of them are below an 18+ launch age, but they are next year's 18–19-year-olds.

### Gaps
- No public TikTok demographic data specific to #OOTD, dupe or styling content was found; TikTok Creative Center data was not retrieved.
- No Morning Consult or Pew data on styling help was retrieved.
- Pacsun/GlobalData's Nov 2025 youth survey was found but not opened: [Pacsun newsroom, Nov 25 2025](https://www.pacsun.com/newsroom/newsroom-When-Youth-Speaks-Culture-Moves.html).
- No 2026 Pinterest Predicts fashion figures were retrieved.
- The fall 2026 Shein/Temu ranking from Piper Sandler is unverified.

## 2. Demographics of fashion/dress-up game players

### Takeaway
Dress-up games skew strongly female and older than people assume. Covet Fashion's historic average player was 29, and 95% of players were women in 2015. Roblox's 18+ cohort is the fastest-growing and monetizes 50% or more better than under-18s, and about 37% of US age-checked Roblox DAU were 18+ in Q3 2026. A claim that 43% of Dress to Impress players are 18+ is unverified.

### Cited Findings
- **Roblox, Q3 2026** (reported Oct 29 2026):
  - Age share: 18+ users were 29% of global and 37% of US age-checked DAU, up from 27% and 33% in Q2 2026.
  - Growth: US 18+ DAU grew 18% YoY and 89% over two years.
  - Monetization: the CEO said 18+ users "monetize 50% or more" than the under-18 cohort.
  - Totals: DAU was 138M (down 9% YoY) and bookings were $1.697B (down 12%). — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-grows-revenues-in-q3-by-16-but-stock-falls-in-after-hours-trading/); [gaming.net](https://gaming.net/roblox-q3-revenue-rises-16-as-bookings-fall-12-to-1-7-billion); [ggrecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/roblox-daily-players-fall-q3-2026/)
- **Roblox age data caveats:**
  - Statista: only 19% of Roblox users were 25+ (Dec 2024). — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1190869/roblox-games-users-global-distribution-age)
  - Konvoy argues kids overstate their ages, which inflates the older cohorts. — [Konvoy, Q2 2025](https://konvoy.vc/content/roblox-is-not-aging-up)
- **Dress to Impress (DTI) adult share (unverified secondary figure):** A French outlet claims 63% of DTI players are over 13 and 43% are over 18, with no source given. — [Next Stage](https://www.next-stage.fr/?p=223212)
- **DTI adult player anecdotes:** Her Campus (about Aug 2024) and college papers such as the Fordham Ram and the Ithacan report adult and college players. Kotaku says DTI is played mostly by children. — [Her Campus](https://hercampus.com/?p=1643721); [Fordham Ram](https://thefordhamram.com/culture/college-students-are-playing-dress-up-again); [The Ithacan](https://theithacan.org/?p=57545); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_to_Impress_(video_game))
- **Covet Fashion:**
  - Age: average player age was 29 and the target was women 25–55 (2015, Fortune). Digiday reported about 70% of players were over 22 (2015). A former senior director later said most players were 35+, so sources conflict.
  - Gender: about 5% of players were men (2015).
  - Scale: about 2M monthly players (claimed, 2020). — [Fortune 2015](https://fortune.com/2015/09/09/covet-fashion-mobile-game/); [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/move-kardashian-mobile-game-striking-chord-women-retailers); [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/?p=34066); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/crowdstar-is-all-in-on-mobile-gaming-for-women-with-covet-fashion/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covet_Fashion)
- **Covet Fashion US revenue (Sensor Tower estimates):**
  - Q1 2024 (iOS): peaked at $371K per week.
  - Q2 2025 (iOS and Android): weekly peak of $199K–$263K.
  - Q4 2025 (iOS): peak of about $113.7K per week. In the same quarter, SuitU (Libii) peaked at about $123K per week, with active users rising from about 64.7K to 111.9K.
  - This implies Covet's US revenue is roughly $5–12M per year (rough inference from the weekly peaks). — [Sensor Tower Q2 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-unified-top-5-fashion%20games-revenue-us-6011f531241bc16eb85bc2b8); [Q4 2025 iOS](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-ios-top-5-fashion-games-revenue-us-6011f531241bc16eb85bc2b8); [Q1 2024](https://sensortower.com/blog/2024-q1-unified-top-5-fashion%20games-revenue-us-6011f531241bc16eb85bc2b8)
- **Zepeto (company and press figures, mostly 2022):**
  - A company executive said the core user base is women aged 13–24.
  - Sensor Tower (Korean blog) named 18–24 and 35–44 as the main age groups.
  - Digiday reported 65% of users are in Asia.
  - Reported user totals: 300–400M registered and about 20M MAU. — [Expanded Ramblings](https://expandedramblings.com/index.php/zepeto-statistics-and-facts/); [Sensor Tower KR](https://sensortower.com/ko/blog/ZEPETO-Avatar-exceeded-200M-downloads-worldwide); [Digiday](https://digiday.com/?p=565975); [Fnac L'Éclaireur](https://leclaireur.fnac.com/article/58720-zepeto-le-metaverse-populaire-chez-les-jeunes-femmes/)
- **Infinity Nikki** (launched Dec 2024):
  - First-month mobile spend was about $16M, about 63% from China (AppMagic estimate, which excludes third-party Android stores in China).
  - Download split: about 41% China and 14% US (otome.com; tracker not named).
  - US revenue per download is about $3, below the global average (Appfigures, Dec 2024). — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/infinity-nikki-styles-up-16-million-in-launch-month-on-mobile); [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20241227/4-infinity-nikkis-global-march); [otome.com](https://otome.com/?p=2131)
- **Quantic Foundry gamer motivation:**
  - Motivations: women over-index on Design (expression and customization), Fantasy, Story and Completion; men over-index on Competition and Destruction.
  - Overlap: even the largest gender difference still has 87% overlap between men and women.
  - Genre share (2017, sample of 270K+, self-selected, core-skewed): Match-3 is 69% female and Family/Farm Sim is near the top; non-binary players over-index in Family/Farm Sim (3.2% vs a 1.1% baseline). — [Quantic Foundry, gender differences](https://quanticfoundry.com/2015/08/28/gender-differences-in-gaming/); [Game Developer, Jan 20 2017](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/beyond-50-50-breaking-down-the-percentage-of-female-gamers-by-genre)

### Inferences
- An 18+ styling game can credibly target women aged 18–35, the same band where Covet historically sat (average age 29) and where Roblox's fastest-growing, best-monetizing cohort is.
- DTI shows Gen Z adults will play dress-up when it is social and has voting. However, the share of DTI players who are 18+ is unknown; 43% is an unverified claim.

### Gaps
- No public figures for DTI's age split from the developer or Roblox.
- No Everskies demographics were retrieved.
- No current (2024+) Covet demographic data.
- No Newzoo data on female or customization players.
- No age-by-willingness-to-spend data for dress-up games specifically; the Roblox 18+ monetization quote is the best proxy.

## 3. New to fashion, budget-constrained, or both?

### Takeaway
It is both, and they overlap. Price leads for most Gen Z shoppers, but anxiety and advice-seeking about outfits are widespread (94% of Gen Z seek work-outfit advice). No published segmentation named "fashion-anxious" or "trend followers on a budget" was found.

### Cited Findings
- **IWG (July 2025):** 94% of Gen Z seek work-outfit advice, versus 84% of millennials, 70% of Gen X and 61% of boomers. About 30% of Gen Z ask their manager. Gen Z and millennials are about 3x as likely as boomers to have outfit anxiety. — [Fortune, Jul 24 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/07/24/gen-z-workplace-outfits-anxiety-layoffs-millennials-pandemic-fashion-boomer-bosses-advice-office-wfh/)
- **H&M/Civey (May 2026; 1,000 German adults aged 18–35):** 75% link fashion to self-confidence, 54% feel judged for their clothes, and 60% pick versatile, easy-to-combine pieces. — [FashionUnited, May 2026](https://fashionunited.com/news/fashion/h-m-2026-study-fashion-between-self-expression-and-social-pressure/2026052272528)
- **YPulse 2026 (US/Canada, ages 13–39):** The summary says most young people "feel they're missing something" when getting dressed; details are paywalled. — [YPulse fashion/style report](https://www.ypulse.com/r/fashion-style)
- **Budget signals:**
  - 66% of Gen Z cite price as a top factor (YouGov, 2026).
  - 70% of teens are pessimistic about the economy, yet higher-income teens drove spending growth (Piper Sandler, fall 2026). — see the section 1 sources
- **Dupe/value behavior (secondary, not verified):** 54% of consumers buy dupes (SoF 2025, via Substack). — [Substack](https://aureliaammour.substack.com/p/fashion-value-driven-shopping-behaviors)

### Inferences
- **Two overlapping needs:** (a) styling confidence or skill ("how do I put this together for my body or the occasion"), sharpest for 18–24-year-olds entering the workforce; and (b) budget ("get the look for less"). Body-type briefs serve (a), and affordable real items serve (b).
- **The 18–24 workforce entrant is a strong wedge:** high outfit anxiety, a new need for a work wardrobe, and low income.

### Gaps
- No public segmentation study with sized segments, such as fashion-anxious or budget trend-followers. The YPulse and BoF full reports may contain one but are paywalled.

## 4. Demand beyond women

### Takeaway
Men's demand is real and growing. Gen Z men are driving Pinterest's male growth, with fashion as their top use case, and r/malefashionadvice has about 5.4–6.5M members. However, men historically make up only about 5% of dress-up game players, so men are a secondary segment and need a different game framing.

### Cited Findings
- **Pinterest men's data:**
  - Men are more than a third of users, with record Gen Z male sign-ups.
  - Fashion is Gen Z men's top use case: 46% used Pinterest for fashion inspiration in the past month.
  - Searches for "men's alternative fashion" rose 185% (first Evolution of Men report, about Aug 2025). — [Mediaweek](https://mediaweek.com.au/pinterest-sees-male-audience-surge-launches-pinterest-man-hub); [B&T](https://www.bandt.com.au/pinterests-evolution-of-men-report-finds-younger-male-users-represent-engaged-growing-cohort/); [Axios, Aug 3 2025](https://www.axios.com/2025/08/03/pinterest-men-trends-pilates-parenting)
- **Pinterest Men's Trend Report 2026** (about Sep 2026; dates across editions conflict): Gen Z men's searches rose 17% YoY and account for more than 62% of male monthly searches. — [Pinterest Newsroom](https://newsroom.pinterest.com/news/men-trend-report-2026/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/pinterest-says-gen-z-men-now-drive-62-of-male-search-activity/)
- **r/malefashionadvice:**
  - About 5.4M subscribers (Wikipedia) or about 6.5M members (Hive Index); counts are approximate.
  - The outfit calculator Twelve70 surged in popularity after being posted on the subreddit.
  - The subreddit's own 2020 survey had 717 responses. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/R/malefashionadvice); [Hive Index](https://thehiveindex.com/communities/r-malefashionadvice/); [2020 survey thread](https://r.darklab.sh/r/malefashionadvice/comments/nargsh/2020_malefashionadvice_subscriber_survey_results)
- **Perry Ellis survey (1,000+ men; date unclear, older):** Getting dressed is hard for many men. — [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/jessica-stillman/men-have-absolutely-no-idea-what-to-wear-new-survey-finds.html)
- **Covet Fashion:** about 5% of players were male (2015). — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/crowdstar-is-all-in-on-mobile-gaming-for-women-with-covet-fashion/)
- **Non-binary players:** non-binary gamers over-index in Family/Farm Sim (3.2%), Casual Puzzle (2.7%) and Sandbox (2.3%), against a 1.1% baseline (Quantic Foundry, 2017). — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/beyond-50-50-breaking-down-the-percentage-of-female-gamers-by-genre)

### Inferences
- Men want styling help (Pinterest, Reddit) but may not want a "dress-up game". Including menswear and gender-neutral briefs widens the funnel at low cost, but marketing should lead with women aged 18–30.

### Gaps
- No data on men's share of DTI or Zepeto players.
- No men's styling app usage numbers.
- No gender-neutral fashion demand survey was retrieved.

## 5. Which countries to launch first

### Takeaway
The US is clearly first. It leads mobile game revenue (about 32% of global mobile game revenue in Dec 2025, per Sensor Tower) and is Covet's core market. The UK, Canada and Australia are the logical English-language follow-ons (inference). Germany is a top-5 mobile game market but not English-speaking. India, the Philippines and Brazil offer downloads but low revenue per user (inference).

### Cited Findings
- **Sensor Tower (Dec 2025):** the US was about 32% of global mobile game revenue, China (iOS only) 13.6% and Japan 12.7%. — [Sensor Tower Dec 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/top-10-worldwide-mobile-games-by-revenue-and-downloads-in-december-2025)
- **Global and growth figures:**
  - Global mobile game IAP was about $81.75B in 2025 (+1.3%), per a secondary source.
  - Spending growth leaders: Turkey +28%, Mexico +21%, India +17%; Japan was down 7%. — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/mobile-game-spending-reaches-818-billion-worldwide-in-2025)
  - An unverified "US $59B" figure appeared in a search summary and is likely wrong; do not use it.
- **Germany:** ranked 5th worldwide in mobile game spending in 2024 (Sensor Tower). — [GamesMarket](https://www.gamesmarket.global/insights-mobile-gaming-market-grew-in-2024-with-germany-ranking-fifth-worldwide-fd737048908424ece95a56448ba016ee/)
- **Sensor Tower publishes per-country fashion-game top-5 posts** (US, GB, Europe, Latin America and others), which confirms fashion games monetize in these markets, but the public posts give no shares. — e.g. [GB Q4 2023](https://sensortower.com/blog/2023-q4-unified-top-5-fashion%20games-revenue-gb-6011f531241bc16eb85bc2b8)
- **Infinity Nikki** is China-led on revenue (about 63%), and the US is a secondary pillar (about 14% of downloads). — see section 2 sources
- **Zepeto** is Asia-concentrated (65% of users). — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/?p=565975)

### Inferences
- **Suggested sequence: US → UK, Canada, Australia → Germany (localized) → Philippines and India as low-cost download and testing markets.** English-language markets let shoppable affordable items (US and UK retailers) work without a product-catalog rebuild.
- **Regulation (from general knowledge, not researched in this pass; verify):**
  - US: COPPA covers under-13s.
  - UK: the Age Appropriate Design Code and Online Safety Act apply age-assurance duties to services likely accessed by children.
  - EU: DSA minor-protection rules apply, and several countries scrutinize loot boxes (e.g., Belgium).
  - Australia: under-16 social media restrictions took effect from Dec 2025; whether they cover games is to be verified.
  - An 18+ gate reduces but does not remove these burdens, because regulators look at "likely to be accessed by children".

### Gaps
- No public Sensor Tower country split for Covet Fashion or Love Nikki.
- No 2025 IAP figures for the UK, Canada, Australia, Brazil, the Philippines or India.
- No Gen Z fashion spend by country.
- The regulatory notes above are unsourced in this research pass.

## 6. Spending power of 18–24 vs 25–30-year-olds on fashion and games

### Takeaway
25–30/34-year-olds have more spending power. Circana reported US 18–24 video game spend fell about 25% YoY in early 2025, while older gamers spent more. 18–24-year-olds still buy in-game content (78% of Gen Z players per the ESA 2026) and spend heavily on clothing. The 18+ monetization premium on Roblox supports targeting adults.

### Cited Findings
- **Circana via PC Gamer (early 2025):** US video game spend by 18–24-year-olds fell nearly 25% YoY. The full article could not be fetched, so this figure comes from a search summary only. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/new-study-shows-that-gen-z-is-spending-way-less-money-on-videogames-than-older-gamers)
- **ESA Essential Facts 2026 (YouGov fieldwork):** 78% of Gen Z players have bought in-game content (via search summary). — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/50-of-gen-z-and-gen-alpha-make-in-game-purchases)
- **CivicScience (older study):** Gen Z mobile gamers are the most willing to make larger IAP purchases, while spending of $5 or less per month is spread evenly across ages. — [CivicScience](https://civicscience.com/?p=23470)
- **TGM 2026 (small sample: n=174 aged 18–24, n=176 aged 25–34):** 25–34-year-olds over-index on footwear and established lifestyle spending. — [TGM Research](https://tgmresearch.com/gen-z-vs-millennials-online-shopping-habits-usa.html)
- **YPulse (Jul 2026):** Gen Z makes more late-night impulse purchases than other generations; clothing and accessories are the top category, per CouponFollow cited in search results. — [YPulse](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2026/07/15/gen-z-makes-more-late-night-impulse-purchases-than-other-gens/)
- **Roblox (Q3 2026):** 18+ users monetize 50% or more than under-18s. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-grows-revenues-in-q3-by-16-but-stock-falls-in-after-hours-trading/)
- **McKinsey (Nov 2025):** Gen Z spend is growing about 2x as fast as other generations. — [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/~/media/mckinsey/email/genz/2025/12/2025-12-02a.html)

### Inferences
- **Monetization vs volume:** expect 25–30-year-olds to carry average revenue per paying user (ARPPU), consistent with Covet's historic average player age of 29, and 18–24-year-olds to carry virality, volume and voting.
- **Monetization design:** low price points and battle-pass or subscription-style offers suit 18–24-year-olds; premium cosmetic bundles suit 25–30-year-olds.

### Gaps
- No clean public split of 18–24 vs 25–30 for apparel spend or mobile IAP; BLS Consumer Expenditure Survey age bands (under 25, 25–34) were not retrieved.
- The Circana figure could not be verified from the full article.
