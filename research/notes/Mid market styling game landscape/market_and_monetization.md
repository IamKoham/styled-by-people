# Market Size and Monetization Benchmarks: Mid-Market Fashion Styling Mobile Game (research as of 21 Nov 2026)

Note on sourcing: most figures were checked through search-result snippets and one full-page fetch (impact.com). Figures marked **[secondary]** come from aggregators or blogs, not primary reports. Figures marked **[unverified]** come from sources that do not show their methodology, or that conflict with other sources. All dollar figures are USD unless stated.

## 1. Market size: mobile games, dress-up subgenre, Roblox fashion/UGC, Gen Z fashion spend, mid-market/fast-fashion e-commerce

### Takeaway
Mobile gaming is large but flat on app-store IAP. Sensor Tower has it at about $82B in 2025 (+1%) and down 2% in H1 2026. Newzoo, which counts more channels, has mobile at $113B in 2025 and forecasts $121B for 2026. The dress-up/fashion subgenre is a small niche: the leading Western title, Covet Fashion, earns only tens of millions a year. The fashion-retail pool that affiliate revenue would draw on is far bigger (Shein alone is about $42B), but affiliate only captures a few percent of it.

### Cited Findings
**Total games / mobile games**
- Newzoo: the global games market closed 2025 at $201.6B (+9.1% YoY), the first time it passed $200B. Mobile was $113.3B (+10.7%), about 56% of the total. Newzoo forecasts mobile at $121.1B in 2026 and the total market at $234.4B by 2028. Earlier Newzoo figures for 2025 were lower: $188.8B in Sep 2025 (mobile $103B) and $197B in Dec 2025, so the estimate was revised several times — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/global-games-revenue-surpasses-200bn-for-the-first-time-mobile-generates-113bn/); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/business/global-games-revenue-breached-200b-in-2025-newzoo/); [PocketGamer.biz, Sep 2025 forecast](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/newzoo-revises-2025-global-games-market-growth-forecast-to-1889bn)
- Sensor Tower (App Store + Google Play only): mobile game IAP revenue was about $82B in 2025 (+1% YoY), from 52B downloads — [Sensor Tower press release](https://sensortower.com/press/press-release-sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-gaming-drove-52-billion-downloads-82b-iap-revenue-on-mobile-and-12b-premium-revenue-on-steam)
- Sensor Tower H1 2026: worldwide mobile game IAP was $40B, down 2% YoY, while downloads fell about 12% to about 24B. Game ad spend rose over the same period — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/mobile-gaming-iap-dipped-2-as-ad-spend-climbed-in-h1-2026/)
- Q2 2026: gaming IAP fell 4.5% YoY, and non-game apps drove the growth in mobile spending — [9to5Mac, Aug 2026](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/11/report-non-gaming-apps-drove-mobile-spending-growth-in-q2-as-games-fell-4-5/)
- July 2026: global mobile game spending was $6.6B per Sensor Tower — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/mobile-games-make-66bn-in-july-as-honor-of-kings-leads-player-spending/)
- Why the sources differ: Sensor Tower counts only App Store and Play Store, so it misses webstore/D2C payments. Newzoo uses a broader methodology, which explains most of the $82B vs $113B gap — [Respawn/Outlook summarizing Sensor Tower](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/mobile-games-82-billion-revenue-2025-sensor-tower-report)
- US-only mobile games market: no reliable 2025/2026 figure was found (see Gaps).

It monetizes mainly through IAP — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/is-glu-mobiles-45-million-crowdstar-acquisition-already-paying-off); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covet_Fashion). SuitU: Fashion Avatar Dress Up (Libii) peaked at about $181K/week unified (early June). In Q4 2025 on US iOS, SuitU peaked at $123K and Covet at $113.7K (early Nov) — [Sensor Tower Q2 2025 unified](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-unified-top-5-fashion%20games-revenue-us-6011f531241bc16eb85bc2b8); [Sensor Tower Q4 2025 iOS](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-ios-top-5-fashion-games-revenue-us-6011f531241bc16eb85bc2b8)

PC, PS5 and China's alternative Android stores are not included, so true totals are higher — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/infinity-nikkis-version-20-update-arrives-as-title-hits-70m-on-mobile-ahead-of-its-first-anniversary); [PocketGamer.biz launch](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/infinity-nikki-styles-up-16-million-in-launch-month-on-mobile)
- Drest (luxury styling game, UK): reported 250% YoY user growth and 260+ luxury brand partners. Beauty Matter (2024) reported "£72.5M ($92M) 2022 revenue", but **[unverified/suspect]**: that figure looks far too high for an app of Drest's size, and it may be a funding or valuation number misreported as revenue — [Beauty Matter](https://www.beautymatter.com/articles/drest-where-luxury-beauty-meets-the-grown-up-gamer)

**Roblox fashion / UGC economy**
- Roblox paid creators about $1.5B in 2025 — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=194006) **[secondary headline]**
- Avatar Marketplace economics: on items sold in the marketplace, Roblox keeps the 40% seller portion plus a 30% market fee, so the creator gets about 30%. If an item is sold inside another experience, that experience's owner collects the 40% — [Roblox Creator Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/economics). Digiday frames the creator share as 30–70% of in-game clothing and accessory revenue, against the 100% Fortnite announced — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/amid-competition-with-roblox-fortnites-in-game-item-strategy-appeals-to-both-creators-and-brands/)
- From 14 Jul 2026, Roblox unified 2D and 3D avatar-item publishing rules and economics. Per a secondhand summary, creators now need ID verification, 2FA and a Plus/Premium subscription to publish — [Roblox DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-stronger-protections-and-a-more-consistent-marketplace-unifying-2d-and-3d-avatar-item-publishing/4722717)
- Individual creator scale: one MENA creator has sold 30M+ avatar items — [Roblox Newsroom, Sep 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/global-impact-of-creation-on-roblox). The creator "Topcat" sells 1M+ items a month (a seven-figure business) — [AOL/BI](https://www.aol.com/pandemic-hobby-7-figure-roblox-173025728.html)
- Dress to Impress (a Roblox fashion runway game, released 11 Nov 2023) grew mainly through TikTok virality. No revenue data was found for it — [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/fashion/article/64382/1/what-is-dress-to-impress-why-is-everyone-so-obsessed-viral-fashion-game-roblox)

**Gen Z fashion spending**
- BCG (Oct 2025; 9,000+ US consumers surveyed plus 50K social posts analyzed): Gen Z and Gen Alpha are projected to account for 40% of fashion spending within a decade, and they spend 7% more of their disposable income on clothing and shoes than earlier generations did — [BCG press release](https://www.bcg.com/press/29october2025-gen-z-gen-alpha-drive-fashion-spending)
- PwC (2025): Gen Z cut overall spending 13% between Jan and Apr 2025, especially on apparel, accessories and electronics. 82% of Gen Z plan to buy less expensive alternatives — [PwC](https://pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/gen-z-consumer-trends.html)
- Morning Consult (Feb 2025): Gen Z apparel shoppers are pulling back because of inflation and show low brand loyalty — [Morning Consult](https://pro.morningconsult.com/analyst-reports/state-of-apparel-shopping)
- Mintel (Dec 2025): apparel spending rises from age 12 and is a top spend category for teen girls — [Mintel](https://store.mintel.com/report/us-gen-alpha-gen-z-fashion-market-report)
- No dollar figure for total US Gen Z apparel spend, and no Piper Sandler Taking Stock With Teens or BoF/McKinsey State of Fashion 2026 figures, were retrieved (see Gaps).

**Mid-market / fast-fashion size**
- Shein: 2025 net revenue was $41.8B (2023–25 CAGR 14.2%) and net profit $2.06B. Its growth is slowing: it added about $9B of revenue in 2024 but only about $3B in 2025 — [FashionUnited, Aug 2026](https://fashionunited.com/news/business/shein-business-model-into-fashions-top-15-at-a-70-discount/2026082974336)
- Inditex revenue is described as "$45B+" in a LinkedIn post **[unverified]**. H&M's gross margin was 53.4% in FY ending Nov 2025 — [Modaes](https://www.modaes.com/global/companies/fashion-in-a-more-expensive-world-industry-giants-have-held-their-ground-and-increased-margins-since-2019)
- Fast-fashion market share (older Coresight data, year unclear): Shein 18%, Inditex 17%, H&M 5% — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/guide/shein-stats) **[secondary]**

### Inferences
- Covet's 2025 US peak of about $263K/week suggests roughly $8–12M/yr in US gross revenue (my own extrapolation, not reported). That is well below its 2019 run-rate of about $67M/yr. Western fashion-styling IAP looks like a mature, slowly shrinking niche, and a new entrant should not assume Covet-scale revenue.
- The pool of dollars for IAP (mobile games, about $82–113B) and the pool for affiliate (fashion GMV, hundreds of billions) are very different in size. But affiliate captures only the commission, about 5–15% of GMV, and only on conversions.
- Gen Z is trading down, which favors the "mid-market/affordable" positioning (Shein, Cider, H&M tier) over premium brands. It also means lower order values and lower absolute commissions.

### Gaps
- US-only mobile games market for 2025/2026: not retrieved. Newzoo and Sensor Tower publish North America splits, but none surfaced.
- Total revenue for the dress-up/fashion games genre (global or US): no source found. Sensor Tower publishes "top 5" lists, not genre totals.
- Revenue for Love Nikki / Shining Nikki, Zepeto, Dress to Impress, Everskies and Highrise: not found.
- Total US Gen Z apparel spend in dollars, Piper Sandler teen survey (fall 2026), and BoF/McKinsey State of Fashion 2026: not retrieved.
- Total fast-fashion / mid-market e-commerce market size: no reliable figure found. Market-research numbers like Statista's were not accessed.

## 2. Game monetization benchmarks (ARPU, ARPDAU, ARPPU, conversion, retention, IAP vs ads, season pass)

### Takeaway
Median mobile retention is low: D1 about 22%, D7 about 4%, D30 under 1%. Top-decile games reach about 40% / 11–12% / 4%. Casual-game payers spend about $7 by D90. A fashion styling game needs top-decile retention and a 2–5% payer conversion to work. Season passes are standard practice, but no reliable adoption-rate benchmark was found.

### Cited Findings
- GameAnalytics 2026 benchmarks (2025 data, all genres): median D1 about 22%, D7 just under 4%, D30 about 0.7–0.8%. Top 10%: D1 about 40%, D7 about 11–12%, D30 about 4%. Top 1%: D1 64–68%, D7 25%+. D1 retention declined from 2024 to 2025 — [GameDevReports summary of GameAnalytics 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-and-pc-game); [GameAnalytics 2026 PDF](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-01-27-2026-mobile-pc-benchmarks_compressed.pdf)
- GameAnalytics Q1 2024 medians: D1 22.91%, D7 4.2%, D28 0.85% — [GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-benchmarks-in-mobile)
- ARPDAU (blended, **[secondary/unverified]**): ads-only casual $0.01–0.05, hypercasual $0.03–0.08, hybrid-casual $0.15–0.50 — [GameGrowthAdvisor, Mar 2026](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-03-17-mobile-game-kpis-benchmarks-2026/)
- ARPPU at D90 (AppsFlyer 2026 data, via summary): casino $11.40, midcore $9.80, casual $7.26 — [GameDevReports: AppsFlyer App Monetization 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appsflyer-app-monetization-in-2026). A blog gives casual ARPPU as $10–30 and mid-core as $50–100, but its time window is undefined **[secondary]** — [Playio](https://blog.playio.co/arppu-mobile-games)
- AppsFlyer: 59% of gaming IAP revenue comes from paid installs, meaning user acquisition drives most game revenue — [AppsFlyer report](https://www.appsflyer.com/resources/reports/app-marketing-monetization-report/?lang=en-US)
- Payer conversion: only the most successful games exceed 1% daily conversion (GameAnalytics H1 2019). Casino converts about 4.95% of installs to payers — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/five-key-takeaways-gameanalytics-h1-2019-mobile-benchmark-report/); [Playio](https://blog.playio.co/arppu-mobile-games) **[secondary]**
- Battle passes, subscriptions and starter packs are being used to convert payers earlier. No adoption-rate statistic was found from a credible source. One aggregator claims passes drove 25% of IAP growth **[unverified]** — [games.gg](https://games.gg/ar/news/mobile-game-monetization-trends-2025/)
- Covet Fashion is IAP-led, and Drest mixes IAP, brand partnerships, advertising and (reportedly) subscriptions — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/is-glu-mobiles-45-million-crowdstar-acquisition-already-paying-off); [Beauty Matter](https://www.beautymatter.com/articles/drest-where-luxury-beauty-meets-the-grown-up-gamer)
- Drest worked with Unity on playable ads, which grew installs-per-mille (IPM) 78% and doubled ad spend. This shows Drest relies on paid user acquisition — [Unity case study](https://unity.com/en/resources/drest)

### Inferences
- Illustrative IAP revenue per install by D90 (my calculation, using the benchmarks above): payer conversion 2–5% × casual ARPPU about $7.26 gives about **$0.15–0.36 per install**. Mature fashion games with dedicated adult female audiences, like Covet and Design Home, likely do better over the full lifetime, plausibly $1–5+ LTV per install (estimate, unverified).
- If engaged DAU yield about $0.15–0.30 ARPDAU (the hybrid-casual range), one engaged monthly user (about 15 active days) is worth roughly **$2–4.50/month** in IAP plus ads. This is a rough estimate.
- Most IAP revenue comes from paid installs, so IAP economics depend on LTV beating cost per install. That is hard for an unproven indie title.

### Gaps
- No genre-specific retention or ARPDAU data for dress-up/fashion games was found. GameAnalytics GameIntel has genre filters but sits behind a login.
- No credible season/battle pass adoption rate was found (commonly cited informally as 5–20% of active users, but unsourced, so not used).
- No IAP-vs-ads revenue split for casual or fashion games was retrieved.

## 3. Affiliate economics

### Takeaway
Fashion affiliate commissions mostly fall between 7% and 20%. Shein offers 10–20%, H&M 7–10.5%, and LTK averages 16%. Affiliate order values are about $114–123, and fashion conversion rates are about 1.5–3%. On those numbers, affiliate revenue per user is likely cents per month, not dollars, unless shopping intent is very high.

### Cited Findings
- impact.com 2025 affiliate benchmark (all verticals): affiliate AOV rose 4% from $118 to $123, while transactions fell 5%. Shoppers clicked 2% more but converted 6% less — [impact.com](https://impact.com/affiliate/affiliate-marketing-benchmark/)
- Awin/Forrester (2024): customers acquired through affiliates have 21% higher AOV than those from other channels — [Awin](https://awin.com/us/affiliate-marketing/15-powerful-affiliate-marketing-statistics)
- Fashion e-commerce conversion: 1.9% (Littledata 2023) and 1.53% (IRP Commerce, May 2026; AOV £91.53) — [Size.ly](https://www.size.ly/blog/fashion-ecommerce-benchmarks). Salesfire reports 5.17% site-wide, an outlier — [Salesfire](https://www.salesfire.co.uk/?p=6667). A blog cites 2.42% conversion and $114 AOV for affiliate fashion traffic, with 1.6% mobile conversion **[unverified]** — [bestcolorfulsocks](https://bestcolorfulsocks.com/blogs/news/fashion-affiliate-marketing-performance-statistics)
- LTK: retailers set the rates, typically 10–25% and up to 30%. LTK reports an average brand commission of 16% and recommends brands offer 14%. One third-party claim says LTK drives $5B+ in annual retail sales **[secondary]** — [Archive.com LTK pricing guide](https://archive.com/blog/ltk-pricing); [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/affiliate-platform-payout-comparison-creators/)
- ShopMy: creator rates run 10–30%. Sacra estimates $1B+ GMV, 1,200 brand partners and about $80M revenue in 2025 **[estimate]**. Its take rate is reported differently by source: about 15% of commission vs up to 2.9% of GMV — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/shopmy); [Stack Influence](https://stackinfluence.com/blog/shopmy-vs-ltk-best-affiliate-platform-2026)
- Shein: 10% base, up to 20% for top affiliates or selected items, with a 30–60 day cookie depending on source — [Creator-Hero](https://www.creator-hero.com/blog/shein-affiliate-program-in-depth-review-pros-and-cons); [Empire Flippers](https://empireflippers.com/fashion-affiliate-programs/) **[secondary]**
- H&M: 7–10.5% (7% existing customers, 10.5% new customers), up to 30-day cookie — [Empire Flippers](https://empireflippers.com/fashion-affiliate-programs/); [ainfluencer 2026](https://ainfluencer.com/fashion-affiliate-programs/) **[secondary]**
- Reformation runs a worldwide affiliate program, rate not published — [Skimlinks](https://www.skimlinks.com/?p=3215)
- Zara/Inditex: no public commission rate was found. One directory lists "based on sales". Inditex is widely understood not to run open affiliate programs, but that was not verified — [UpPromote directory](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/shein/)
- impact.com marketplace examples (2026): DTC fashion brands pay 10–20%, e.g. Magda Butrym 10%, Margovil 15%, Birdies 10–15%, Fabletics 17% on new VIP. Cookies are typically 30 days — [impact.com fashion programs](https://impact.com/affiliate/fashion-affiliate-programs/)
- Amazon Associates fashion/apparel: commonly cited at about 4%. This comes from prior knowledge of Amazon's fee schedule and was **not re-verified this session** — [Amazon fee schedule](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/schedule)

### Inferences
- Illustrative affiliate revenue per monthly active user (my model):
  - Conservative: 5% of MAU click through to a retailer in a month × 2% conversion × $110 AOV × 10% commission gives about **$0.011/MAU/month**.
  - Optimistic: 20% click × 3% conversion × $120 AOV × 15% commission gives about **$0.11/MAU/month**.
  - Net of network or platform fees and returns, which run high in fashion and void commission, the real figure is lower.
- Compared with IAP's rough $2–4.50 per engaged MAU, affiliate is likely 1–2 orders of magnitude smaller per user. It works better as a feature that adds credibility and a selling point for brand deals than as the main revenue line. Note that shopping apps like LTK and ShopMy earn their scale through creator traffic with very high purchase intent, which a game will not have.
- Brands that pay new-customer premiums, like H&M's 10.5% for new customers, fit a Gen Z acquisition story well.

### Gaps
- Commission rates for Zara/Inditex, Aritzia, Abercrombie, Urban Outfitters, Madewell, Mango, ASOS, Princess Polly, Edikted and Cider were not found in this session. Rates on Rakuten, CJ and Awin are generally visible only after a publisher logs in.
- No credible "affiliate revenue per user" benchmark for content or shopping apps was found.
- No game or app that publicly reports affiliate revenue from in-game items linked to real products was found.

## 4. Brand-sponsored game content: examples, pricing, ROI measurement

### Takeaway
Brand activations in games are common: 600+ brand campaigns on Roblox in 2025, 60+ of them in fashion. But fees are almost never disclosed. Agency estimates run from low five figures for an item drop to six or seven figures for a custom world. ROI measurement is still weak, and the measurable metrics are visits, engagement minutes and sales of virtual items.

### Cited Findings
- Gamefam: over 600 brand games and campaigns launched on Roblox in 2025, up from 400 in 2024. Fashion accounted for 60+ activations, including Adidas, Nike, Coach and Ralph Lauren — [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/411480/); [Yahoo/Gamefam](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/gamefam-report-reveals-winning-metaverse-153257136.html)
- Pricing **[agency estimates, unverified]**: a branded item drop inside an existing popular experience starts in the low five figures. A full custom world like Vans World or Gucci Garden costs six figures and up, and can reach seven figures with ongoing updates. One analysis uses a $1M build cost as its example — [Influencers-Time Roblox playbook](https://www.influencers-time.com/roblox-brand-playbook-build-creator-experiences-that-drive-r/)
- Gucci Garden drew 19.9M visits from 17 to 31 May 2021. A virtual Gucci bag resold for over $4,000 — [Roblox Gucci case study](https://corp.roblox.com/wp-content/assets/pdfs/Gucci_case_study.pdf); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/someone-spent-over-dollar4000-on-this-gucci-bag-in-roblox). Vans World had 100M+ visits in its first year — [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/gucci-vans-roblox-scavenger-hunt/647686)
- An Adidas digital necklace on Roblox sold for 2M Robux (about $20K) — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/from-metaverse-platform-to-ugc-platform-how-robloxs-virtual-brand-activations-are-building-a-robust-creator-economy)
- Roblox does not take a cut of fees brands pay to creators who build experiences for them. Top Roblox creators don't treat brand deals as a significant revenue stream — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/top-roblox-creators-dont-view-brand-deals-as-a-significant-revenue-stream/)
- ROI: Fortnite's in-game sales of branded items give advertisers the most direct conversion metric so far. Publicis's Digitas called it "a clearer ROI path" — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/amid-competition-with-roblox-fortnites-in-game-item-strategy-appeals-to-both-creators-and-brands/). The Interline says that without robust measurement, Roblox has stayed "a creative outpost, not a core part of media plans" — [The Interline](https://www.theinterline.com/?p=27517)
- Coach ran experiences on both Roblox and Zepeto to test demand for digital Coach pieces. No Zepeto fee or revenue data was found — [Jing Daily](https://jingdaily.com/posts/roblox-fortnite-zepeto-which-offers-brands-more-bang-for-their-buck)
- Drest's brand model: sponsored challenges or "quests" with brands such as Gucci (launch partner, 2019), Cartier, Jimmy Choo and Manolo Blahnik (2025), across 260+ brands. Fees not disclosed — [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/mobile-fashion-gaming-is-gaining-traction/); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/drest-delves-fine-jewelry-watches-040145584.html); [FashionNetwork](https://us.fashionnetwork.com/news/New-styling-app-drest-combines-gaming-with-ecommerce,1144232.html)
- Balenciaga x Fortnite (2021) is the reference case for a connected physical and digital drop — [Coresight](https://coresight.com/research/roblox-and-fortnite-bridges-to-the-metaverse-for-fashion-brands/)

### Inferences
- A small indie styling game will not command Roblox-scale fees, because its reach is far smaller. A plausible early price is a few thousand to low tens of thousands of dollars per sponsored challenge, comparable to a mid-tier influencer campaign. That estimate is extrapolated, not sourced.
- Brand deal revenue is lumpy. It scales with audience size and with how well the game can prove purchase intent. Affiliate click and conversion data from the game could serve as that ROI proof, which is a reason to build affiliate even though its direct revenue is small.

### Gaps
- No disclosed deal values were found for Covet Fashion brand challenges, Drest, Forever 21 Shop City, Zepeto or Fortnite fashion collaborations.
- No standard CPM or CPE (cost per engagement) benchmarks for in-game brand integrations were found.

## 5. Trend data as a product

### Takeaway
The leading trend-forecasting firms (WGSN, Heuritech, EDITED) don't publish prices and sell custom enterprise contracts. One informal source puts WGSN at tens of thousands of dollars a year. No reliable market-size figure, and no evidence of a game selling player style data to brands, was found. This revenue line is speculative.

### Cited Findings
- WGSN does not publish pricing; buyers contact sales. Commentary puts its subscription at "tens of thousands of dollars" depending on company size and tier **[informal]**. A claim of $3,000/yr from a low-quality page should be disregarded — [WGSN](https://wgsn.com/en/wgsn); [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/wgsn/reviews)
- WGSN has revamped its platform as part of a growth drive and offers forecasting tools like TrendCurve for buyers — [BoF](https://www.businessoffashion.com/articles/global-markets/bof-exclusive-wgsn-debuts-revamped-platform-growth-drive/); [WGSN TrendCurve](https://wgsn.com/en/trading/trendcurve)
- Heuritech prices "tailored" plans for each brand and sells Fashion Insights Reports. No figures are public — [Heuritech pricing](https://heuritech.com/pricing/)
- CB Insights compares Heuritech and WGSN as competitors — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/heuritech-vs-wgsn)
- INSEAD case study: WGSN is described as the world's largest trend-forecasting agency and is adopting AI — [INSEAD](https://publishing.insead.edu/case/wgsn-and-trend-forecasting-fashion-and-beyond-putting-ai-technology-work?src=author)

### Inferences
- Buyers like WGSN customers pay five-figure annual subscriptions. A styling game would compete with Heuritech's social-image AI and EDITED's retail-pricing data, both of which have scale advantages. Game data on what players choose is novel, but it is a small, possibly biased sample until the game has millions of users.
- Selling insights derived from minors' data raises COPPA and GDPR-K compliance issues, since much of the dress-up audience is under 18. Restricting data products to 18+ players, or packaging the insight with a sponsored-challenge deal rather than selling it standalone, is likely safer. This is analysis, not legal advice.

### Gaps
- No published pricing for EDITED, Trendalytics or Pinterest Predicts (which Pinterest gives away free as a marketing report) was retrieved.
- No market-size figure for trend forecasting or fashion analytics was found.
- No example was found of a game or app selling aggregated player style data to fashion brands.

## Key question: revenue per user by stream, and which should be primary

### Takeaway
On the evidence above, IAP is the only stream that plausibly reaches dollars per engaged user per month. Brand deals are the second lever, but they are lumpy and depend on audience scale. Affiliate is likely cents per user, and trend data is near zero until the game has scale. IAP should be primary, with brand deals secondary. Affiliate and data are best treated as features that support and differentiate brand deals.

### Cited Findings
- Casual D90 ARPPU of $7.26, and only the most successful games exceed 1% daily payer conversion — [GameDevReports/AppsFlyer](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appsflyer-app-monetization-in-2026); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/five-key-takeaways-gameanalytics-h1-2019-mobile-benchmark-report/)
- Affiliate AOV $123 with falling conversion; fashion conversion about 1.5–2% — [impact.com](https://impact.com/affiliate/affiliate-marketing-benchmark/); [Size.ly](https://www.size.ly/blog/fashion-ecommerce-benchmarks)
- Covet Fashion, the closest comparable, is IAP-led, while Drest layers brand partnerships on top of IAP — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/is-glu-mobiles-45-million-crowdstar-acquisition-already-paying-off); [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/mobile-fashion-gaming-is-gaining-traction/)

### Inferences (all estimates from my own model; rough orders of magnitude)

| Stream | Rough revenue per engaged MAU per month | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| IAP (+ light ads) | $0.50–4.50 (casual ARPPU × 2–5% payers; hybrid ARPDAU) | Medium: benchmark-based, not genre-specific |
| Affiliate | $0.01–0.11 | Medium: arithmetic from sourced commission, conversion and AOV inputs |
| Brand-sponsored challenges | $0 until about 100K+ MAU; then roughly $0.05–0.50/MAU at a few deals per quarter | Low: no disclosed deal sizes |
| Trend data | about $0 until millions of users; then a few five-figure contracts | Low |

- Implication: at 100K MAU, IAP might yield about $50K–450K/month, while affiliate yields about $1K–11K/month. Brand deals could plausibly add $10K–50K per deal once the game shows measurable engagement and conversion.
- Retention is the main risk. The median game keeps under 1% of players to D30. The founder should model the top decile (about 4% D30) as the target, not the base case.
