# Competitive Landscape: Fashion Styling / Dress-Up Games (as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-21. Most third-party revenue and user figures are estimates from trackers (AppGoblin, profitable.app, RoWatcher, Sensor Tower blog snippets, otome.com). They are marked as such. Many figures are older; dates are given inline.

## Competitor profiles (owner, launch, model, metrics, audience, brands, mechanics, strengths/weaknesses, complaints, corporate events)

### Takeaway
The category splits into four types. (1) Real-brand challenge-and-vote mobile games: Covet Fashion (EA) and Drest (luxury). (2) Roblox UGC hits: Dress to Impress, with about 10B visits. (3) Big-budget Chinese gacha dress-up: the Nikki series (Papergames/Infold). (4) Avatar social platforms: Zepeto, Everskies, Roblox brand worlds. No sourced 2026 entrant combines real mid-market items, real prices, voting and affiliate shopping. Kim Kardashian: Hollywood shut down in April 2024, and Covet appears to be in late-life decline.

### Cited Findings

**Covet Fashion (Crowdstar → Glu Mobile → EA)**
- Ownership: Covet was one of Glu's big games, alongside Design Home and Kim Kardashian: Hollywood. GamesBeat said Covet and Design Home "opened up new markets for games targeted at women." — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/ea-completes-2-4-billion-acquisition-of-glu-mobile/)
- EA's Glu deal (completed April 2021): $2.4B equity value, about $2.1B enterprise value. — [Fenwick](https://www.fenwick.com/insights/experience/fenwick-represents-glu-mobile-in-2-4-billion-acquisition-by-electronic-arts); [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210429005646/en/Electronic-Arts-Completes-Acquisition-of-Glu-Mobile-Creating-a-New-Global-Leader-in-Mobile-Gaming)
- After the acquisition, Glu titles showed "revenue losses across the board" except MLB (data.ai, Q1 2021 vs Q1 2022). — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/acquisition-retrospecitve-ea-glu/)
- May 2025: DressX launched a fully virtual capsule collection inside Covet, with select items also sold on DRESSXME.com. EA calls Covet a styling platform with "10+ years of success" in its Lifestyle portfolio. — [DressX](https://dressx.com/news/dressx-and-ea-mobile-s-covet-fashion-reveal-partnership-to-redefine-in-game-style); [Business Wire May 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250515113577/en)
- Metrics (third-party estimates):
  - AppGoblin: 16.2M installs, 323K ratings, 260K–417K MAU (two snapshots that disagree), "$10K+" monthly revenue, with an estimated mix of 24% IAP and 76% ads. The revenue figure looks like a floor bucket and is likely an underestimate. — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/620112416); [AppGoblin trends](https://appgoblin.info/apps/620112416/trends)
  - Sensor Tower (Q2 2025, US iOS fashion games): Covet's weekly revenue peaked at about $199K in mid-May 2025, which implies roughly $0.6–0.9M per month in US iOS alone. That is my extrapolation, not a reported figure. — [Sensor Tower blog](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-ios-top-5-fashion%20games-revenue-us-6011f531241bc16eb85bc2b8)
- Monetization: US App Store IAP packs run about $0.99–$27.99, including a "Super Model" pack at about $19.99. There is a $5.99 "Premium Fashion Pass." — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id620112416); [EA Forums](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/other-ea-games-en/formal-request-for-resolution/12850002)
- Real-world shopping: the listing says items link to retailers so players can buy them in real life. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id620112416)
- Budget mechanic: reviewers describe challenges with spending caps, such as $200 or $500 versions, and say costs keep rising. Another reviewer says you can win without paying by mixing and matching items. — [App Store listing/reviews](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/covet-fashion/id620112416)
- Body diversity: Apple editorial says Covet has models of different body types and ethnicities. The main listing emphasizes hair and makeup diversity. — [Apple story](https://apps.apple.com/us/story/id1497022178)
- Complaints:
  - Voting tiers are set by closet value, so they favor high-level players with better hair and makeup. — [JustUseApp reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/620112416/covet-fashion-dress-up-game/reviews)
  - Billing glitches with no refunds. EA support said it couldn't access Covet systems. — [EA Forums](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/other-ea-games-en/formal-request-for-resolution/12850002)
  - A petition asks Covet to hire player-designers. — [Change.org](https://www.change.org/p/glu-covet-hire-players)

**Drest (Lucy Yeomans; luxury)**
- Leadership: founded 2019 by Lucy Yeomans (ex-Net-a-Porter and Harper's Bazaar). In 2022 Lisa Bridgett became CEO and Yeomans moved to co-chair and chief brand/product officer. — [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/people/fashion-game-drest-names-new-ceo-as-founder-steps-into-new-role/2022021161272); [TheIndustry](https://www.theindustry.fashion/drest-appoints-lisa-bridgett-as-new-ceo/)
- Funding: raised £15M in June 2023 for "DREST 2.0," built on Unity. — [TheIndustry.beauty](https://theindustry.beauty/luxury-beauty-and-fashion-game-drest-continues-momentum-securing-15m-funding/); [FashionNetwork Jun 2023](https://us.fashionnetwork.com/news/Drest-raises-more-funding-to-develop-metaverse-enabled-version-2-0,1529118.html)
- Relaunch (Nov 2024): rebuilt on Unity after a roughly 6-month hiatus. It moved from iOS-only to global iOS and Android under the tagline "Exclusive for All." — [PocketGamer.biz, Mar 7 2025](http://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-drest-relaunched-on-unity-to-merge-fashion-with-gaming/)
  - New features: Narrative Quests, VIP Events, a magazine-style home feed, **model avatars in different body sizes**, and weekly leaderboards.
  - Revenue is "largely" brand partnerships and activations.
  - Brands: more than 260 luxury brands, including Gucci, Valentino, Prada, Cartier, Versace and Manolo Blahnik.
  - Campaign try-ons: 1.5M+ Versace items and 4M+ Manolo shoes tried on.
  - About 60 staff. No user or revenue numbers disclosed.
- Original model (2019–2020):
  - Drest was a Farfetch affiliate, taking a commission per transaction, and Farfetch handled the in-app purchases.
  - Players got a virtual budget and could buy more at $3.99 per $5,000 bundle.
  - It had about 160 fashion partners in 2020.
  - BoF framed the bet as whether people would pay for virtual goods ("fashion's FarmVille").
  - Sources: [TheIndustry launch](https://www.theindustry.fashion/drest-the-worlds-first-interactive-luxury-styling-game-launches/); [FashionNetwork](https://us.fashionnetwork.com/news/New-styling-app-drest-combines-gaming-with-ecommerce,1144232.html); [BoF](https://www.businessoffashion.com/articles/technology/a-new-game-could-be-fashions-farmville-will-people-pay-to-play)
- The 2025 relaunch article does not mention Farfetch or shopping integration. It only says players "might later" buy items in real life. — [PocketGamer.biz](http://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-drest-relaunched-on-unity-to-merge-fashion-with-gaming/)

**Dress to Impress (Roblox)**
- Developer and launch: Dress To Impress Group, led by "Gigi," who started the game at 14. Released November 11, 2023. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_to_Impress_(video_game))
- Mechanics:
  - Players have 360 seconds to build a themed outfit, using up to 18 items.
  - Outfits walk a runway and other players rate them 1–5 stars. The top 3 go to a podium.
  - Also: duo mode, rank-based item unlocks, and a freeplay mode.
  - Items are bought with in-game dollars or Robux, or unlocked with limited-time codes.
  - Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_to_Impress_(video_game))
- Visits and players:
  - About 10.4B total visits as of July 7, 2026, and 10.5B for the group. — [profitable.app](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/dress-to-impress); [profitable.app developer](https://profitable.app/roblox/developers/dress-to-impress-group)
  - About 9.7B visits; all-time peak of 1.5M concurrent; 651K concurrent during the Charli XCX "Brat" event (Aug 2024); averaging 250K+ concurrent in Sept 2024; currently averaging 37K–54K. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/dress-to-impress-how-a-teen-developer-s-fashion-game-hit-9-7-billion-visits)
  - The steep fall to about 37K–54K average concurrents suggests the game is past its peak.
- Revenue (estimates only; Roblox does not publish per-game revenue):
  - profitable.app: $83.3M lifetime and $1.2M over the last 30 days (as of July 2026).
  - Another tracker: about $820K per month (stated at 61% confidence).
  - A 2026 ranking: $2–5M in Q1 2026.
  - Sources: [profitable.app](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/dress-to-impress); [RoWatcher top-earning 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-10-highest-earning-roblox-games-in-2026-and-what-they-mean-for-the-platform)
- Collaborations: Charli XCX "Brat" (Aug 2024), Lady Gaga "Mayhem" (Aug 16–23, 2025), Wicked: For Good (Nov 8–29, 2025), a Flee the Facility crossover (Jan 2026), and a PhatMojo physical toy line with DLC codes. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_to_Impress_(video_game)); [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/dress-to-impress-how-a-teen-developer-s-fashion-game-hit-9-7-billion-visits); [Gameranx](https://gameranx.com/updates/id/543645/article/roblox-dress-to-impress-getting-real-life-dolls-for-fans-to-own/)
- Demographics: Kotaku and the NYT say the game drew older teens and young adults into Roblox, helped by streamers such as Madison Beer, Pokimane and Kai Cenat. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_to_Impress_(video_game)). For Roblox overall, one report cites a near 50/50 gender split among gamers. — [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/fashion-brands-are-competing-for-roblox-creators/)
- Complaints:
  - Spite-voting (giving one star regardless of the outfit).
  - Friend-group clique voting.
  - Pay-to-win perception around VIP items.
  - Server crashes at code drops.
  - The Lady Gaga meet-and-greet backlash.
  - Removal of a community manager.
  - Source: [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/dress-to-impress-how-a-teen-developer-s-fashion-game-hit-9-7-billion-visits)
- 2024 creator controversy: the creator of rival game *It Girl* accused Gigi of fatphobic comments and of a DMCA takedown attempt. Others made further accusations, which Gigi denied. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_to_Impress_(video_game))
- I found no acquisition reported. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_to_Impress_(video_game))

### Inferences
- Covet's estimated ad share (76% on AppGoblin) is not reliable. Covet is IAP-led, and Sensor Tower's roughly $199K weekly US iOS peak contradicts the "$10K+/month" figure. Treat AppGoblin revenue as a floor bucket.
- Dress to Impress shows that timed theme plus runway plus peer star-voting is a viral loop for teen girls. It also shows that open peer voting produces spite and clique problems at scale.
- The Nikki backlash shows that Gen Z/female players are vocal and organized about monetization changes. Piece-splitting and paywalled customization are red lines.

## Which competitors link to real-world shopping, and how well does it work/convert?

### Takeaway
Only Covet (retailer links), original Drest (Farfetch affiliate with commission) and Zepeto (Walmart e-commerce, 2025) have documented real-shopping links. None has published a conversion rate. Drest's 2025 relaunch appears to have moved away from shopping toward brand-sponsorship revenue, which suggests affiliate revenue alone was not enough.

### Cited Findings
- Covet: items link to retailers to buy in real life. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id620112416)
- Drest: launched as a Farfetch affiliate taking a commission per transaction, with Farfetch processing purchases. — [FashionNetwork](https://us.fashionnetwork.com/news/New-styling-app-drest-combines-gaming-with-ecommerce,1144232.html); [TheIndustry](https://www.theindustry.fashion/drest-the-worlds-first-interactive-luxury-styling-game-launches/)
- Drest 2025: revenue is "largely" brand partnerships, and real-world buying is only "might later." — [PocketGamer.biz](http://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-drest-relaunched-on-unity-to-merge-fashion-with-gaming/)
- Zepeto: Walmart ran Zepeto's first e-commerce experience in 2025. — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/what-marketers-need-to-know-about-zepeto-the-korean-metaverse-platform/)
- DressX x Covet: items were available both in-game and on DRESSXME.com, but these are digital rather than physical goods. — [DressX](https://dressx.com/news/dressx-and-ea-mobile-s-covet-fashion-reveal-partnership-to-redefine-in-game-style)

### Inferences
- The lack of any published conversion data after 6+ years (Drest 2019 to present) and Drest's pivot suggest affiliate conversion has been weak. Luxury price points (Farfetch) likely limited purchases by young players. A mid-market price band is a plausible differentiator, but it is unproven.

### Gaps
- No conversion or attach-rate data found for any title.
- Whether Farfetch's 2023–2024 financial troubles and its sale to Coupang ended the Drest integration is unconfirmed in sources fetched.

## What do players love and hate (pay-to-win, voting fairness, item prices, energy limits)?

### Takeaway
Players love creative self-expression, themed challenges, real or celebrity brands, and runway voting. They hate unfair voting (spite, cliques, tiering by closet value), pay-to-win items, escalating prices, and monetization changes that paywall customization.

### Cited Findings
- DTI: spite-voting, clique voting, and VIP pay-to-win concerns. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/dress-to-impress-how-a-teen-developer-s-fashion-game-hit-9-7-billion-visits)
- Covet: voting tiers by closet value favor payers; challenge costs keep rising; billing and refund failures. — [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/620112416/covet-fashion-dress-up-game/reviews); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/covet-fashion/id620112416); [EA Forums](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/other-ea-games-en/formal-request-for-resolution/12850002)
- Infinity Nikki: outfit piece-splitting, paywalled dyes, furniture pricing, and the girlcott. — [Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/infinity-nikki-bubble-season-1-5-update/); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/open-world/infinity-nikki-devs-sincerely-apologize-yet-again-this-time-for-frustration-and-confusion-caused-by-furniture-pricing-but-at-least-were-getting-freebies-in-return)
- Kim Kardashian: Hollywood: energy-gated progression monetized through K-Stars and ads. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Kardashian:_Hollywood)
- Players also want co-creation: the Covet petition to hire player-designers. — [Change.org](https://www.change.org/p/glu-covet-hire-players)

### Inferences
- Voting integrity is the top recurring pain point across titles. Possible defenses include weighted or reputation-based votes, anonymized outfits, and vote-quality scoring.

### Gaps
- I found no sourced energy-limit complaints specific to Covet. Direct Reddit sampling was not done.

## Has anyone done budget-based or "dupe" challenges with real prices?

### Takeaway
Covet has the closest analog: challenges with spending caps such as $200 or $500, using real-brand items. Original Drest gave a virtual budget in in-game dollars. I found no game that runs explicit "dupe" challenges (find the cheaper lookalike) at real 1:1 retail prices.

### Cited Findings
- Covet challenge spending caps ($200 or $500 versions) that reviewers say keep rising. — [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/covet-fashion/id620112416)
- Drest: a virtual budget, with $5,000 top-ups sold for $3.99. — [FashionNetwork](https://us.fashionnetwork.com/news/New-styling-app-drest-combines-gaming-with-ecommerce,1144232.html)

### Inferences
- "Dupe" and budget-to-real-price mechanics look like open whitespace. One caveat: Covet's in-game item prices are not confirmed to equal real retail.

### Gaps
- I could not verify whether Covet item prices mirror real retail prices 1:1.

## How do they handle body diversity in avatars?

### Takeaway
Body diversity is limited and recent. Drest added model avatars in different body sizes in its Nov 2024 relaunch, and Covet offers models with varied body types and ethnicities. In the DTI community, fatphobia surfaced as a controversy.

### Cited Findings
- Drest 2024 relaunch: model avatars in different body sizes. — [PocketGamer.biz](http://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-drest-relaunched-on-unity-to-merge-fashion-with-gaming/)
- Covet: in-game models of different body types and ethnicities. — [Apple editorial](https://apps.apple.com/us/story/id1497022178)
- DTI: the 2024 It Girl creator accused Gigi of fatphobic comments (Gigi denied it). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_to_Impress_(video_game))

### Inferences
- Real-item games face a production cost for size inclusion, since each garment must be rendered on multiple bodies. This may explain the slow adoption.

### Gaps
- I found no sourced detail on body options in the Nikki series, Everskies, Zepeto or Roblox avatars.
